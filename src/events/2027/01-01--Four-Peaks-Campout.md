This can be a day trip or a campout, depending on your preferences!
Details below.

Rick will lead a run up and over Four Peaks starting Friday morning. The group
will have lunch at the top and make their way down the far side. For anyone who
only wants to do a day trip, they can depart here or from the top and go home.
For the rest who want to camp, Rick will find a camping spot for the group.

There is no official activity planned for Saturday or the rest of the weekend
while camping, but the group will probably find something to do!

If you have questions, reach out to Rick for details or come to the club
meetings!

### Requirements

* [Normal required gear](/about/required-gear)
* [Tonto OHV Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/tonto/passes)
