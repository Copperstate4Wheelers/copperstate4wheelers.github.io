This is expected to be an easy (if long) day starting south of Buckeye,
following the Agua Caliente Road all the way down to Sentinel on route 8. Along
the way, we'll pass through Sundad, an old abandoned site of some sort, pass
the Gila Bend mountains and the 4th of July Butte, and visit Agua Caliente
itself (an old ghost town).

Bring lots of water, we'll be out in the sun.

### Requirements

* **State Trust Land Permit Required**
* [Normal required gear](/about/required-gear)
